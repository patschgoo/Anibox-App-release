AniBox for Android 0.3.0 (test version)

- Play on TV (CastApp): a show page has "Play on TV" when CastApp is on the phone. The episode and the ones after it go to CastApp's "Cast to TV" sheet, with their subtitle files and your place. CastApp's reports save your place, and AniBox's own rule (95 % or the last 20 seconds) decides "watched".
- On the Shield: setup offers "Use this device's own drives". Android asks once for "all files access", and then no password is needed. The sync folder and the episodes are read straight from the drive.
- Downloads: an episode's menu has "Download to this phone", which keeps the episode with its subtitle files. A download that stops goes on where it stopped, and the player uses a download first, so it plays without the share.
- On mobile data, the player says how big the original file is and asks once before playing it.
- Who's watching (Settings): the PC's profiles without a PIN, each with its own places. Kids profiles see only their shows.
- The next episode is ready during the countdown. Headset and lock-screen buttons control the player. Picture in picture works when you press Home.
- The debug report names shares and addresses only as host1/share1.

Still signed with the public test key. Not tried on a real phone, Shield or CastApp yet.
